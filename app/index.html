Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, no mission replay, cheats disable trophies. Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter. Click any trophy chip to open its guide.

⚠ One trophy can be missed: — meditate with Lucia on Jason's balcony in Chapter 1, right after the mission Down to Earth. It is the only thing in the game that might lock you out of the platinum.
